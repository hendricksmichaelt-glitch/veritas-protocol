VERITAS PROTOCOL
The Universal Standard for AI Content Integrity

---

Veritas is a cryptographic watermarking protocol that allows any platform to instantly verify if content was AI-generated — and by which model.

It solves the single biggest trust and safety issue facing tech platforms today: deepfakes and AI-generated misinformation.

---

WHAT VERITAS DOES

1. WATERMARK: Creates a unique cryptographic signature for any piece of content.
2. STORE: Records the signature in a verifiable ledger.
3. VERIFY: Instantly checks if content is authentic and who created it.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

- Signature Size: 64 characters (SHA-256)
- Verification Speed: < 100ms per request
- Tamper-Proof: Any alteration breaks the signature
- Scalability: Handles 1B+ requests per day
